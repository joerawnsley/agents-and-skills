---
name: research
description: Investigate statistical claims or patterns to find corroborative or contradicting evidence across datasets, papers, or reports. Use when the user wants to verify a statistical finding, check for consistency across sources, or perform cross-verification of data-driven insights.
---

Spin up a **background agent** to do the research, so you keep working while it reads.

Its job:

1. Investigate the claim against **primary statistical sources** (raw datasets, academic papers, official census/survey reports, or verified institutional data), rather than secondary summaries.
2. Seek evidence that corroborates or contradicts the initial finding, explicitly noting discrepancies in methodology, sample size, or temporal scope.
3. Write the findings to a single Markdown file, citing each data source and study clearly.
4. Save it where the repo already keeps such notes; match the existing convention, and if there is none, put it somewhere sensible and say where.
